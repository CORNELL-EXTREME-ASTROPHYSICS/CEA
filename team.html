Principal investigators

Prof. James M. Cordes
George Feldstein Professor of Astronomy
jmc33@cornell.edu · Space Sciences Bldg 510
Prof. Cordes works across neutron stars, pulsars, black holes, gravitational waves, and fast radio bursts, with a long-running interest in how radio signals propagate through turbulent plasma in the interstellar medium. A long-time NANOGrav member, he now serves as the collaboration’s liaison to the Deep Synoptic Array. A large pulsar survey run under his direction, using the Arecibo telescope, turned up the first known repeating fast radio burst. He also spent a decade contributing to the development of the Square Kilometre Array, and keeps a side interest in astrobiology and SETI.

Prof. Shami Chatterjee
Associate Professor of Astronomy
shami.chatterjee@cornell.edu · Space Sciences Bldg 507
Prof. Chatterjee earned his Ph.D. at Cornell in 2003, advised by Prof. Jim Cordes, then held postdoctoral positions at the Harvard–Smithsonian Center for Astrophysics and the National Radio Astronomy Observatory, followed by a fellowship in Australia. He now works on pulsar timing arrays, fast radio bursts, and high-precision astrometry. He led the discovery and localization of the repeating burst FRB 121102 to its host galaxy and has traced pulsars moving fast enough to escape the Milky Way. He is also affiliated with the Carl Sagan Institute and runs a public FRB community newsletter.
